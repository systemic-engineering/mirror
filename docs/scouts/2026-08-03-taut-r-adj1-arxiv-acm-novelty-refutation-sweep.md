## §2 Findings

### §2.1 Zero papers satisfy all four conjuncts simultaneously

The four conjuncts under test (math §7.5 post-refactor per Reed R3 REED-INLINE
`a6d6243`):

- **(i)** design tokens compiled from a substrate-decl'd typed grammar of ≥ 6
  family-root carriers with named versioning discipline;
- **(ii)** render pipeline composed as substrate-native cascade with loss-lens
  measurement between named source + target grammars;
- **(iii)** delivery pipeline substrate-decl'd as a typed family with
  content-addressed image assembly + multi-target dispatch;
- **(iv)** two-channel indissolubility preserved at every pipeline stage per
  Watzlawick 1967 axioms 1+3+5 (formalized, not merely aesthetic;
  structural-property-shaped at deploy altitude; in-tick-firing-shaped at
  render altitude).

**Refutation surface: EMPTY.** No paper returned across 14 queries names or
describes a system satisfying all four conjuncts simultaneously.

### §2.2 What the queries did return

Broadly three classes:

1. **ML/RL cascades** (Nemotron-Cascade, Cascade R-CNN, Cascade-DETR,
   Polyharmonic Cascade, UCCI cascade routing, Power Failure Cascade, LM
   cascades): all use "cascade" in the model-composition or inference-routing
   sense, not the substrate-native-render-pipeline sense of conjunct (ii). Zero
   overlap with typed design-token grammars or Watzlawick pragmatics.

2. **CI/CD + build-provenance papers** (Kettle attested builds, STRIDE CI/CD,
   OpenSource CI/CD for SDVs, Xanadu serverless): touch conjunct (iii)-adjacent
   delivery-pipeline concerns but treat the pipeline as *build-tooling
   metadata*, not as *substrate-decl'd typed family*. No content-addressed
   image assembly + multi-target dispatch as a typed-family-primitive. No
   loss-lens. No design-token grammar.

3. **Two-channel / dual-channel frameworks** (MTD, DC-Net sarcasm, WADBERT,
   3DMolFormer, Two-Channel Allen-Dynes): all use "two-channel" in the
   ML-feature-decoupling sense (shared/private feature extraction, literal/
   implied sentiment), not the Watzlawick relational/content sense of conjunct
   (iv). Zero pragmatics-of-communication overlap.
