## §3 — The four properties per PAPER §3.6 formalized (sheaf-cohomological grounding)

### §3.1 Setup: cellular sheaf on a shard-graph

A **shard-graph** `G = (V, E)` at prismqueer altitude has vertices V = shard-family-roots and edges E = composition-shard-body dispatches. A **cellular sheaf** `F` on G (per Curry 2014, Def. 2.2.1) assigns:

1. **Vertex stalks** `F(v)` — a vector space at each vertex v ∈ V. At prismqueer altitude: `F(v) = StateVector` per `prismqueer::coincidence::StateVector` (sparse-basis Hilbert-carrier in a named space, grep-verified).

2. **Edge stalks** `F(e)` — a vector space at each edge e ∈ E. At prismqueer altitude: `F(e) = ComposeHistoryVector` (sparse-basis Hilbert-carrier tracking the compose-history-of-restriction-maps for the edge).

3. **Restriction maps** `F_{v⊴e} : F(v) → F(e)` — linear maps from vertex-stalks to edge-stalks whenever v is incident to e. At prismqueer altitude: `F_{v⊴e}` is a `prismqueer::Prism` per the Prism trait (focus/project/settle), landed at `prismqueer/src/lib.rs`.

**Coboundary operator** (Curry 2014, Def. 2.4.1). For a chosen orientation of edges (canonicalized via lower-OID → higher-OID per `edge_key` convention, grep-verified in insight `dirac-operator-on-graphs.md` §3):

$$
(\delta x)(e) = F_{v_1 \trianglelefteq e}(x(v_1)) - F_{v_2 \trianglelefteq e}(x(v_2))
$$

for an edge `e = (v_1, v_2)` with `v_1 < v_2` in OID order.

**Sheaf Laplacian** (Hansen-Ghrist 2019, Def. 3.1). The sheaf Laplacian is:

$$
L_F := \delta^* \delta
$$
