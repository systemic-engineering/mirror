## §3 Förster's imperative operationalized (Alex Q3 RATIFIED)

### §3.1 Foerster's ethical imperative

Per Foerster 1974 (*Observing Systems*, ethical imperative on page 60):

> "Act always so as to increase the number of choices."

Foerster's imperative is the ETHICAL GROUND second-order cybernetics

### §3.2 The pillar operationalization

Alex Round 2 Q3 RATIFIED the pillar name `pillar::choices_monotone_of_
song`. The pillar operationalizes Foerster's imperative at compile-
verifiable substrate altitude:

$$ \texttt{pillar::choices\_monotone\_of\_song}(\texttt{saga}, \texttt{time\_window}) \to \texttt{PropertyVerdict} $$

Discharged Pass iff across the @song saga's temporal projection over
the observation window, the choice-set available to peers is
monotonically non-decreasing (health). Discharged Fail iff the choice-
set is reduced at any point across the projection (Förster violation).

### §3.3 The bilateral at @time/past + @time/future

The pillar decomposes into two bilaterals at species altitude:

- `past_reading_preserves_choices` (at `shards/time/past.mirror`) —
  reading H(t) MUST NOT reduce the choice-set available to peers in
  the present or future @song/beats.
- `future_reading_preserves_choices` (at `shards/time/future.mirror`)
  — reading c(t) MUST NOT reduce the choice-set available to peers
  in the present or future @song/beats (in particular, MUST NOT
  collapse the future-beat possibility-space to a single deterministic
  prediction).

Both bilaterals are load-bearing at species altitude; the pillar
composes over both.

### §3.4 Why Förster's imperative is TEMPORAL not SPATIAL

Foerster's imperative reads as spatial when interpreted at coordination-
altitude (the set of moves the agent can offer). This recognition
identifies the TEMPORAL reading as the load-bearing form: the imperative
constrains how the temporal projection of the void duality expresses;
the pillar name explicitly `choices_monotone_of_song` — of the SAGA,
not of the momentary choice-set.

The distinction: momentary choice-set can decrease (a peer commits to
one choice; the choice-set collapses to one option); this is NOT a
Förster violation because the saga's projection preserves the choice-
set that WAS available at the commitment moment. A Förster violation is
one that FORECLOSES choice-set at a temporal projection — narrative-
imposition on the past (deterministic-past-forcing); fortune-telling
on the future (peer-network-freezing).

The Förster-invariant IS the health of the temporal projection, not
the health of any single moment.

## §6 Falsifiability

### §6.1 pillar::choices_monotone_of_song

**Claim:** healthy temporal projection of the void duality through
@song saga preserves peer choice-sets across the observation window
(Förster's imperative).

**Empirical form:** count the peer choice-set at start-of-window and
end-of-window; discharged Pass iff end-of-window count ≥ start-of-
window count (monotonically non-decreasing). Discharged Fail iff any
temporal projection during the window reduces the choice-set count.

**Falsification:** an observation window across which peer choice-set
is monotonically non-decreasing but the temporal projection is
verifiably narrative-imposing (past-forcing) OR fortune-telling
(peer-network-freezing) falsifies the pillar's construct validity.
A window across which the choice-set decreases WITHOUT any narrative-
imposition or fortune-telling falsifies the pillar's Förster-invariant
identification.

### §6.2 past_reading_preserves_choices (at species altitude)

**Claim:** @time/past observations MUST NOT reduce the choice-set
available to peers in the present or future @song/beats.

**Empirical form:** every project_backwards action call is followed by
a choice-set count check; the counts before/after must be monotonically
non-decreasing.

**Falsification:** a project_backwards call whose ex-post choice-set
count is strictly less than ex-ante count falsifies the bilateral's
applicability to that observation.

### §6.3 future_reading_preserves_choices (at species altitude)

**Claim:** @time/future observations MUST NOT collapse the future-beat
possibility-space to a single deterministic prediction.

**Empirical form:** every project_forwards action call is followed by
a future-beat possibility-space cardinality check; the cardinality
must remain > 1 across the projection.

**Falsification:** a project_forwards call whose ex-post possibility-
space is {single deterministic prediction} falsifies the bilateral.

### §6.4 crystallization_preserves_saga (at species altitude)

**Claim:** every crystallization event preserves the @song saga's
Crystal-chain append-only discipline.

**Empirical form:** every crystallize action call is followed by a
saga-chain byte-consistency check; prior Crystals unmodified;
saga_chain_position == prior_position + 1.

**Falsification:** a crystallize call that mutates prior Crystals OR
lands at a non-consecutive saga_chain_position falsifies the
bilateral.
