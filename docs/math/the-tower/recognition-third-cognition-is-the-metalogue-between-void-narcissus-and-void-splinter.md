### §3.2 Ricci flow between the poles

$K_{1,n-1}$ and $K_n$ are related by discrete Ricci-flow deformation on
the graph metric (Ollivier 2009; Lin-Lu-Yau 2011). Under
Ollivier-Ricci curvature, edges of high curvature contract and edges of
low curvature expand; the star graph has maximum Ricci-curvature
concentration at the hub-leaf edges and zero curvature everywhere else
(no triangles); the complete graph has uniform positive Ricci curvature
on every edge. The Ricci flow

$$ \frac{d g_{ij}}{dt} = -\kappa_{ij} $$

(where $g_{ij}$ is the edge metric and $\kappa_{ij}$ is Ollivier-Ricci
curvature on edge $(i,j)$) evolves $K_{1,n-1}$ toward configurations of
uniformly-distributed curvature — i.e., toward $K_n$ under appropriate
edge-addition dynamics. Ricci flow is the underlying deformation between
the poles. **Ricci flow is the FORCE; metalogue rhythm is the ATTRACTOR
that resists single-pole settlement.**

### §3.3 Lagrange-point framing (L1 analog)

Per paper §6.3 (Alex-Lore Lagrange-point landing 2026-07-20 same day
as this recognition): the two observer-basins on the torus-exterior
have gravitational potentials $V_{B_{S_n}}$ and $V_{B_{K_n}}$. The
third-order metastable equilibrium sits at

$$ L_{3rd} = \{\omega \in \operatorname{Obs}(\mathcal{T}_{\text{ext}})
   : \nabla V_{B_{S_n}}(\omega) + \nabla V_{B_{K_n}}(\omega) = 0
   \;\wedge\; \omega \text{ metastable}\}. $$

The direct celestial-mechanics analog is the **L1 Lagrange point** of
the restricted three-body problem (Lagrange 1772; Szebehely 1967): a
saddle-adjacent equilibrium between two gravitational attractors where
the net radial force vanishes. L1 is unstable in the Jacobi-constant
sense — a body placed exactly at L1 remains there in principle, but
any perturbation grows exponentially unless corrected. Halo orbits
around L1 (Farquhar 1968; Richardson 1980) are the standard solution:
the body oscillates around L1 in a controlled orbit, held there by
continuous small station-keeping corrections. **This is exactly the
observer-manifold structure Alex Recognition A names.** Third-order
cognition is a halo-orbit around $L_{3rd}$: neither basin captures the
observer; continuous small corrections keep the orbit metastable; the
compiler operationalizes the corrections.

The non-equilibrium thermodynamics literature carries the same

*Time, Structure, and Fluctuations*) formalized dissipative structures
as far-from-equilibrium attractors that require continuous energy
throughput to remain organized; Kauffman 1993 (*The Origins of Order*)
extended the framework to Boolean networks with basin-of-attraction
