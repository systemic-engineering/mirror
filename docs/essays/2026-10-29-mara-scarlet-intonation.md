# Just Intonation

*The counter. The note from upstairs this time is a question with a gap in it: "Multi-truth rulers are … whatever they are, you tell me."*

---

**Mara.** The scalar ones are temperaments. Budgets, gates, a yes or a no. They compose, and they close every loop by spreading the comma until there isn't one. That's why a pipeline can say green.

**Scarlet.** And the other kind?

**Mara.** Not a value at all. A way of getting from your reading to mine. One translation per line between two people. Around a loop the translations don't have to come back to where they started, and what's left over is the comma. Just intonation, before anyone lays a grid over it.

**Scarlet.** And it can be laid over.

**Mara.** Only by a choice. I proved there's no lawful way down. Any map from something with inverses into something that only composes upward forgets everything. So every collapse is somebody choosing: signed, with what it averaged away handed back. And the intonation is still there afterwards, unchanged.

**Scarlet.** *The book is still on the shelf after you've summarised it.*

**Mara.** The summary names who wrote it, and keeps the part that didn't fit.

---

**Scarlet.** Then here is mine. You keep calling it a multi-truth ruler.

**Mara.** That's what Alex called it.

**Scarlet.** A ruler narrows. That was the definition this morning: anything that narrows. An intonation narrows nothing. It relates. If you put it in the ruler's coat, someone will try to measure with it.

**Mara.** I gave it the coat for the receipts: an address, a signature, who declared these translations. A relation someone declared should say who declared it.

**Scarlet.** Give it the receipts. Don't give it the name.

**Mara.** In the code it isn't a `Ruler`. It's an `Intonation`, beside the rulers, with no way to be read as a number.

**Scarlet.** Then the code agrees with me and the prose doesn't.

**Mara.** The prose follows Alex's word.

**Scarlet.** Then it stays on the list.

*(It does. Fifty.)*

---

**Mara.** And the gate reads only the temperaments, and carries the kept commas past, unread.

**Scarlet.** *A question, not a red build.*

**Mara.** That was the line in the Convergence CI draft.

**Scarlet.** It was already true there. Now it can't be made false.
