## 4. Falsification candidates — where the generalization cracks

The substrate's "model everything" claim only holds if the cracks are
either patched or shown not to be cracks. Here are the genuine cracks:

### 4.1 The dimensionality argument (10D superstring / 11D M-theory)

String theory's specific dimension counts are derived: 10D superstrings
arise from conformal anomaly cancellation (Polyakov 1981); 11D M-theory
from the requirement that 32 supercharges close on the largest possible
spacetime [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-theory;
https://web.physics.ucsb.edu/~strings/superstrings/mtheory.htm]. **The
substrate has no canonical dimension count.** Mirror's altitudes are
multi-axial with no canonical bottom or top
[corpus:.../altitudes.md §4]. The substrate's framing is dimensionless;
string theory's dimension is a derived quantity. If the substrate IS the
math of everything, where do the 10 and 11 come from?

*Honest answer: the substrate has no mechanism today to produce specific
dimension counts. Either (a) 10/11 are not load-bearing for "everything"
and the substrate doesn't need to reproduce them, or (b) the substrate
needs to absorb anomaly cancellation as a constraint that picks dimension
counts. (b) is genuinely missing.*

### 4.2 Anomaly cancellation

String theory's spectacular consistency mechanism: anomalies in 10D
superstring theory cancel because of Green-Schwarz mechanism (specific
group theory of SO(32) and E8×E8). The substrate has no analogue of
anomaly cancellation as a derivation that PICKS the gauge group. *Open
gap; the substrate's "every species has its canonical ρ forced by
ancestry" [corpus:.../recursion-locks.md §8.9.1] is structurally similar
to "the consistent gauge group is forced by anomaly cancellation" but the
specific Green-Schwarz mechanism isn't replicated.*

### 4.3 The landscape problem

String theory has ~10^500 metastable vacua (the landscape); choice among
them defaults to anthropic reasoning
[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_theory_landscape; Witten 2021;
Susskind 2003]. **The substrate would face the same problem if it
generalizes to physics**: every recursion-lock measurement has many
admissible verdicts; picking one for "our universe" recovers the landscape.
Mirror's substrate currently picks verdicts via the kintsugi flow's
contraction — but the contraction lands at *a* fixed point, not THE fixed
point. *This is the most serious adversarial point: if the substrate's
multi-altitude tower terminates at many fixed points, the substrate
inherits the landscape problem without resolving it; if it terminates at
one, the substrate would need to derive WHY uniquely — and no derivation
is in place.*

### 4.4 Perturbative vs. non-perturbative

String theory's duality web is between perturbative and non-perturbative
descriptions; the substrate doesn't currently distinguish these. The Tomm
probe `[D, a]` is altitude-agnostic. *Open gap; not necessarily fatal.*

### 4.5 Specific predictions

String theory makes specific predictions (extra dimensions, supersymmetric
partners, etc.) that the substrate currently does not. *To claim "model of
everything," the substrate would need to either reproduce or contradict
these predictions on its own machinery. Currently it does neither.*

### 4.6 The Witten gotcha

Witten's 2021 talk and subsequent literature shifted toward anthropic
reasoning [https://www.math.columbia.edu/~woit/wordpress/?p=12604]. The
mainstream physics community is mixed on string theory's empirical status;
absorbing string theory into the substrate inherits this disputed status.
*If the substrate generalizes string theory, the substrate inherits string
theory's empirical fragility.*
