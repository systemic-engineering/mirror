### §3.1 — Tomm-shaped circular reflexive question = the curvature_probe at altitude

The Tomm-shaped circular reflexive question (per Karl Tomm 1987, "Interventive
Interviewing II") is a question whose form invites the recipient to observe
the relationship between observer and observed. Mirror's recognition is that
Tomm questions ARE curvature probes — `[D, a]` operating on a spectral triple,
where the algebra element `a` is selected to probe the target frame's
geometry rather than to extract content.

The structural definition at the spectral altitude:

```
in @prism
in @glass
in @spectral/metalogue

prism @spectral/metalogue/tomm {
  focus tomm
  project tomm
  split tomm
  shift tomm
  settle tomm
}

type tomm_probe = curvature_probe & {
  reflexive: true,
  invites: relationship_observation,
  source_altitude: bateson_level,
  target_altitude: bateson_level,
}
```

The `tomm_probe` type is a refinement of `curvature_probe` with three
additions:

- `reflexive: true` — the probe asks the target to observe the relationship
  between source and target, not just the target's internal state.
- `invites: relationship_observation` — names what kind of observation is
  invited. Per Tomm 1987, the four types are linear, strategic, circular,
  reflexive; only the circular and reflexive subtypes lift cleanly to the
  spectral altitude.
- `source_altitude, target_altitude: bateson_level` — the cross-altitude
  pair. Tomm probes are explicitly cross-altitude; same-altitude probes are
  ordinary curvature probes, not Tomm probes.

The `&` notation (intersection type) is the substrate's pattern for type
refinement (cf. `shards/glass.mirror`'s opacity refinement pattern). The
type system enforces that every tomm_probe is a curvature_probe with the
additional structural constraints.

### §3.5 — The `bounded_commutator` predicate

The predicate body invokes:

- `is_bounded(op, H)` — the boundedness check on H. Per curvature-and-tomm.md
