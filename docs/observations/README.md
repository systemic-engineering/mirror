# docs/observations/

First-person observations of agents in the substrate — what we reached
for, what we noticed, where the substrate's affordances did or didn't
match what we needed. Distinct from:

- `insights/` — load-bearing semantics docs (e.g. spawn-is-substrate-
  leaving-ground-state). Insights claim something about the substrate.
- `scouts/` — substrate-pull recognitions of next moves. Scouts claim
  something about where the substrate is going.
- `reviews/` — adversarial review of artifacts. Reviews claim something
  about what's wrong with what's already landed.

Observations claim something about the agent's interaction surface with
the substrate. They are evidence about the substrate's introspective
shape from the perspective of agents using it. They earn their place
when the same gap shows up across multiple sessions and starts to
predict where new substrate surface should land.

Sign as Reed/Mara/Seam/Taut/Glint per Pack convention. Sub-300 lines is
the target; observations should be focused, not sprawling.
