## The temporal eigensheaf — math-rock's substrate contribution

Classical music's bar-line structure quantized rhythm into a single metric layer. Math-rock + African polyrhythm theory + statistical-mechanical models of meter together surface the temporal axis as a first-class eigensheaf:

- **St. Clair & Berezovsky, *Rhythm as an ordered phase of sound* ([arXiv:2604.07476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07476), April 2026)** — meter literally emerges as an ordered phase transition in a statistical-mechanical free-energy minimization. Validated against Bach. The temporal analogue of harmonic settling.
- **Schwabedal, Knapper, Shilnikov ([arXiv:1509.04514](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.04514))** — three coupled Fitzhugh-Nagumo oscillators give up to **5 simultaneously stable polyrhythmic states**. Polyrhythm = multistable phase coexistence. This grounds Tool's *Lateralus* 9/8–8/8–7/8 alternation, Meshuggah's polymetric cycles, and Battles' loop layering as substrate phenomena.
- **Tool's *Lateralus*** — Fibonacci syllable counts + golden-ratio-scaled restriction maps between adjacent temporal scales. The temporal eigensheaf isn't just integer-period; it has Fibonacci-ratio nesting structure.
- **Krebs's metric dissonance** (*Fantasy Pieces*) — *displacement dissonance* (phase offset) and *grouping dissonance* (incommensurate periods) are **exactly the two restriction-map failures of a sheaf**: phase failure (transition cocycle not 0) and grouping failure (no common refinement).
- **Agawu, African polyrhythm** — the timeline (Z₁₂ or Z₁₆ bell pattern) IS the base sheaf; polyrhythmic voices are sections over it.
- **London, *Hearing in Time*** — meter as entrainment; resonance at `λ_t`.

The substrate needs `@epistemologic/math/music/temporal_eigensheaf` as a first-class declaration alongside the harmonic eigenspaces. Math-rock proves it through bands that *only* settle rhythmically.
