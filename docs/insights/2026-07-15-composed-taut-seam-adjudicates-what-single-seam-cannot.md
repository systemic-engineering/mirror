# Composed Taut→Seam adjudicates what single-Seam cannot

*Session-level substrate discipline finding, 2026-07-15. Grounded in the
@kintsugi/ouroboros → @sheaf → @secrets → A1 cascade Alex's /loop
drove today (32 commits).*

## The claim

Alex's `/loop` directive was: **"collapse until unresolvable ambiguity
that cannot be adjudicated with a Seam spawn."** The pattern this
session revealed: **"Seam spawn" admits composed cascade chains as
legitimate adjudication paths, not just single-Seam-alone.**

The A1 arc empirically demonstrates this:

1. Seam composition-alignment audit at `cec55a2` explicitly triaged A1
   as **"genuinely unresolvable at Seam altitude."** Prior Seam verdict:
   two-tick discipline (land Position (a) status quo; defer Position (b)
   until Taut scouts precedent).
2. Reed re-interpreted Seam's own recommendation: the Taut precedent
   scout Seam itself named IS still Seam-adjudicable work at the second
   tick.
3. Taut precedent scout at `9a5502a` gathered grep-first empirical
   evidence across nine @io family species: 1/14 narrative-shorthand vs
   13/14 landed-constructor; 8 of 9 species with zero narrative-shorthand;
   direct sibling precedent (crypto.ssh_key_material) + sub-species
   precedent (sops.sops_key_group_from_sheaf_restriction) landed SAME DAY.
4. Seam re-adjudication at `e5d928e` with Taut precedent as ground truth
   **SUPERSEDED the prior "unresolvable" verdict.** SEAM-RATIFY Position
   (b) landing as Landing 6. Explicit R4 finding: raw 7% below Seam's own
   >20% threshold, BUT distribution collapses to singleton at the site
   under adjudication — the threshold's presumption of low-mass
   distribution fails empirically.
5. Mara Landing 6 at `64b0438` discharged the ratified substrate-decl
   action `key_material_ref_of(peer: ref) -> imperfect { \ }`.

## What the pattern names

**Single-Seam-spawn adjudicates within Seam's own knowledge horizon.**
When the adjudication requires *empirical evidence Seam does not carry*
(e.g., "is this narrative-shorthand pattern common or exceptional across
the substrate?"), single-Seam-spawn correctly triages as
"unresolvable-at-Seam-altitude" — not because the question is
fundamentally Alex-only, but because Seam lacks the *evidence* to
adjudicate.

**Composed Taut→Seam adjudicates via evidence-grounded adjudication.**
Taut's grep-first read-only substrate scout produces empirical evidence
Seam can then re-adjudicate against. The trade-off between competing
abstract principles (`[[feedback-craft-not-deliver]]` vs
`[[feedback-no-rust-extension-shortcut]]` in this case) *becomes
empirical* once precedent-of-precedent is measured.

## When to compose Taut→Seam

When Seam triages an item as "genuinely unresolvable at Seam altitude,"
check the triage rationale:

- **Rationale = empirical-evidence-gap** (Seam needs precedent Seam does
  not carry): **compose Taut→Seam.** Spawn Taut for precedent-of-precedent;
  respawn Seam with Taut's scout as ground truth. The re-adjudication
  will often supersede.
- **Rationale = Alex-authority-question** (family-root mint, recognition
  naming, initial framing): **escalate to Alex.** Precedent evidence does
  not disambiguate authority.
- **Rationale = fundamental-Rice-limit** (whole-tick decidability
  boundary): **truly Alex-only.** Neither Taut nor Seam can decide;
  Alex holds the framing.

The A1 case was rationale (empirical-evidence-gap): Seam had two coherent
abstract positions and could not empirically choose between them. Taut's
evidence made the choice.

## Substrate-honest bound

**This is not a general recursion license.** Composed Taut→Seam is
substrate-honest ONLY when:

1. The Seam "unresolvable" triage explicitly names precedent-gathering
   as the missing input (Seam recommended Taut in the A1 case verbatim).
2. Taut's evidence is grep-first, read-only, empirically verifiable, and
   cites landed substrate by path + line number.
3. The re-adjudication is at *the same Seam altitude* (Phase D adversarial
   review), not at a meta-altitude auditing Seam's own audit process.
4. The evidence CAN plausibly resolve the ambiguity (not just add noise).

Condition 3 is load-bearing: Foerster's refusal-of-tower (per
`shards/torus.mirror` p. 238) refuses meta-meta observers. Composed
Taut→Seam is **heterarchical** (two Pack peers at their own altitudes,
collaborating), not **tower-building** (a meta-Seam observing Seam's
failure). The distinction matters.
